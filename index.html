📝 Smart Notes

A beautifully designed notes and calculator app with a secret — your private workspace for social media, hidden behind a simple PIN.

About Smart Notes

Smart Notes is more than just a notebook. On the surface, it's a polished notes app with a built-in calculator. But enter your secret 6-digit PIN on the calculator and press "=", and a hidden workspace opens — giving you instant access to all your social platforms in one private, secure place.

📝

Smart Notes

Create, edit, pin, and organize notes with categories. Word count, search, and sort. A fully functional notes app that's genuinely useful.

🧮

Working Calculator

A real calculator with full arithmetic support. Type your secret PIN and press "=" to unlock the hidden workspace. No one will know.

🔒

Private Workspace

Access 30+ social platforms — Facebook, Zalo, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Discord, TikTok and more — all in one secure, hidden space.

🛡️

Auto-Hide Security

The hidden workspace instantly disappears when you turn off your screen or switch apps. No one can see what you were doing.

🌍

21 Languages

English, Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Arabic, and more. Auto-detects your device language.

Fast & Lightweight
