MediaVault

Ultra-Fast Sub-Second Encryption for Personal Photos, Videos & Audio

GET IT ON Google Play
DOWNLOAD ON App Store

Traditional vault apps freeze your phone and overheat the battery when encrypting large 4K videos or music libraries. MediaVault introduces an innovative Partial Header Encryption & Obfuscation engine in a custom .mvlt binary container. Large multi-gigabyte media files lock in milliseconds with zero CPU strain.

⚡

Sub-Second Header Encryption

Secures file signatures with AES-256-CTR and XOR-obfuscates raw bytes. Encrypts a 5GB 4K video instantaneously.

🔐

Hardened PBKDF2 Key Derivation

Derives strong encryption keys with 10,000 iterations and 16-byte random salts. Immune to rainbow-table and brute-force attacks.

🖼️

In-Memory Dynamic Previews

Generates thumbnails and streams media directly in RAM. Zero unencrypted temporary files ever touch physical disk storage.

🎬

Integrated Media Viewer Suite

Who is on your Wi-Fi? NetDiscovery provides complete visibility into your local wireless and wired networks. Combining a high-speed ARP/ICMP scanner with automated IEEE OUI manufacturer resolution, port analysis, and multi-protocol discovery, NetDiscovery turns your mobile device into a professional network audit station.

📡

Real-Time Radar Visualization

Mesmerizing pulsing radar interface visualizes active subnet sweeps across 254+ IP ranges with live response counters.

🏷️

Open Port Diagnostic Scanner

Lost the living room remote again? RemoteTV turns your smartphone into a sleek, ultra-responsive smart controller. Utilizing the latest Android TV Remote Service (v2) protocol alongside mDNS auto-discovery and TLS socket security, RemoteTV delivers instant command response without plastic clutter or dead batteries.

⚡

Zero-Configuration mDNS Discovery

Automatically discovers TVs broadcasting _androidtvremote2._tcp on your local Wi-Fi within seconds.

🔒

Secure One-Time TLS Pairing

Exchanges cryptographic pairing certificates with your TV once. Subsequent reconnections happen instantly without PINs.
